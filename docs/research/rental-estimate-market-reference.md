## Presentation and maintenance

- Show the period beside the references, not only an unqualified “last year.”
- Suggested introduction: «برای انتخاب نرخ، تغییر قیمت چند دارایی در یک سال را ببینید.»
- Suggested explanation: «این درصدها تغییر قیمت گذشته به تومان هستند؛ پیش‌بینی سال آینده یا نرخ تورم نیستند.»
- Bitcoin is an example of a cryptocurrency, not a crypto-market index.
- A reference can fill the draft annual-rate field; applying the estimate remains
  an explicit action. Keep the Renter free to choose a different expectation.
- Use the dated snapshot until the values, endpoints, and sources are researched
  together again. Do not relabel old values as live data.
- Percentage changes are identical in rial and toman because both endpoints use
  the same conversion factor. No fees, spread, or transaction costs are deducted.

All price observations above come from the publisher of the quoted series, TGJU;
they were not independently reconciled against executed exchange trades.
